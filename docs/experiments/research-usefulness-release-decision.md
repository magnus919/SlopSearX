# Bounded opt-in release decision — proposed, not approved

Status: proposed release bar awaiting maintainer decision. This document
registers no acquisition or model run and changes no current acceptance gate.
The full production objective and implementation scope remain unchanged.

## Candidate and decision boundary

Freeze the exact EXP-076 canonical complete-pool query/purpose transaction and
equal-weight fusion as the sole candidate for one new fresh end-to-end study.
Do not change questions, criteria, representation, weights, model or projection
after registration. Historical EXP-076 remains rejected under its original
requirements. A new study is not a rerun of its exposed cases.

A passing fresh study selects this candidate for full opt-in implementation
and fresh confirmation under the adopted protocol, followed by compatibility,
security, integration, review and actual product PR delivery. Existing query-only
behavior remains the default. This is not a smaller prefix feature or permission
to bypass required production work.

## Proposed practical release bar

The proposed change is to require at least +.02 mean nDCG@10 under each separate
reference, with the same positive 95% bootstrap lower bound, instead of the
current +.05. This change is **not approved**. It trades a larger card-ranking
margin for an explicit demonstrated task-use benefit. No historical outcome is
regraded and no output is analyzed against a silently changed threshold.

For eight new primary research tasks and new naturally acquired pools, require
under each independently frozen assessor:

- At least two additional completed tasks relative to W0 under equal opening,
  context, answerer and time/token budgets. Report paired wins/ties/losses and
  uncertainty; this small cohort supports a bounded opt-in claim, not population
  generalization or an unconditional production-default recommendation.
- No task completed by W0 becomes incomplete under the candidate.
- No material unsupported claim, omitted critical contradiction/qualification,
  target/version error or unearned completion assurance in candidate answers.
- Every declared critical check must have traceable, applicable evidence that
  is delivered and correctly used. Correct abstention is reported separately
  and does not count as task completion.

Keep per-case ranking noninferiority (-.03), useful counts, facet retention,
repeat/rotation overlap (.8), present exact-target top1, complete eligible
membership, all resource/latency/usage guards and atomic fallback. Require four
naturally acquired primary pools of 41–80 eligible cards; fewer is inconclusive.
Keep every fixed case, failed/empty/overflow acquisition and missing target;
no rescue searches, substitutions, synthetic pool concatenation or trimming.

Before acquisition, freeze exact new tasks/targets/critical checks, engine/query
plan, numerical gates, full source/answer packet assignments and physical-call
budgets. Freeze independent source references before selector/answer outputs.
Invalid or unassessable required packets make the decision inconclusive; no
repair/retry or averaging away assessor disagreement.

## Stop conditions

Exactly one registered candidate, one fixed cohort and one invocation. A failing
or inconclusive study does not trigger automatic tuning or a replacement run.
Retain the evidence and make an explicit reassessment. No runtime implementation
claim follows from validator fixtures, catalog integrity or a documentation merge.

Remaining Brave allowance stays reserved (8/10 used); plan the study with a
fixed non-Brave engine mix. No search, source capture, model or provider call
is authorized by this proposal alone.
