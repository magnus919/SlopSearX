# Frozen gate calculator preparation

The deterministic calculator applies the approved mixed-workload gates to closed reference and answer assessments and explicit operational observations. Independent A/B ranking and R1/R2 task-use decisions remain separate. Missing required evidence makes the overall result inconclusive; known failed gates remain visible.

The calculator checks source-stage and packet-stage identities separately and binds the selector to the reference closure receipt. It requires explicit engine accounting, including a Brave count; absent measurements never become zero. Bootstrap calculations preserve the frozen recipe.

Validation: 229 tests and 9 subtests passed across the coverage-study and intent-ranking tests. The 14 focused calculator tests cover real closure output shapes, stage and receipt mismatches, missing resource observations, Brave accounting, and mixed incomplete/failed decisions. Ruff and whitespace checks passed. Focused tests were run without the repository-wide coverage threshold; they do not establish whole-repository coverage.

This is preparation only. No search, Jev, capture, answerer, or grading provider calls were made. Synthetic fixtures establish calculation behavior, not research quality. The calculator neither admits a study nor authenticates external measurements. Actual capture transport qualification, operational evidence collection, and fresh development/confirmation results remain required before product delivery.
