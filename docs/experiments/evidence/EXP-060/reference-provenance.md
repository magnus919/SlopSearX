# Fresh independent references

Two GPT-6 Luna reviewers each assessed all 396 cards across thirteen cases. Cards were independently shuffled with seeds 91960 and 91961 and replaced by per-query aliases. Reviewers received only their shuffled public cards, the fixed purpose/facet vocabulary and EXP-039 annotation protocol. They did not receive native rankings, alias maps, model outcomes or the other reviewer’s labels. Work was completed in four bounded batches per reviewer; serialization supported individually authored judgments, not automatic classification.

Both complete sets were checked for exact inventory, unique labels, six-field schema, integer grade ranges, allowed facets and nonempty rationales. Three unsupported or overstrong A-side claims were corrected by that reviewer before model calls, with the prior batch retained privately and the exact changes recorded in reference-A-audit.md. A preliminary B output using broad defaults was discarded and never used in the assembled references; all final B rows come from the individually authored batch files.

These are independent assistant judgments, not human gold labels, an adjudicated consensus or evidence that unseen pages substantiate their cards. Reading-lead grades and visible-information grades remain distinct. Both references are evaluated separately under the frozen analysis.
