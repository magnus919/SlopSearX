# Research brief: useful evidence rather than incumbent identity

Research question: how should future selector acceptance protect research
quality while allowing replacement of useful incumbent URLs?
Depth: bounded methodological review of retained project evidence.
Commissioned by: maintainer decision on 2026-10-05.

## Findings and confidence

- High confidence in the bounded observation: EXP-076 replaced useful sources
  with other useful sources; counts/facets and stability passed, but original
  identity and average-quality gates failed. This does not prove task uplift.
- High confidence in the process need: the source-use pilot and reference-format
  comparison separate attribution from task completion; neither demonstrated
  completion improvement. Blind ratings and uncertainty must remain visible.
- Decision, not empirical finding: future acceptance prioritizes source-bound
  task usefulness, coverage and contradictions rather than preserving URLs.
  The exact next candidate and numerical task-use gates remain unregistered.

## Evidence and limits

| Evidence | Contribution | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| EXP-076 completed/readout.md and analysis.json | fixed quality and retention outcomes | exposed development cohort |
| EXP-076/source-retention-diagnosis.md and .json | source ranks/grades and substitutions | descriptive, not causal |
| source-use-next-stage/pilot-outcome.md | actual source-use and attribution gaps | two exposed tasks, reviewer disagreement |
| evidence-id-ablation/readout.md | matched reference-format result | no task-completion gain |

These are project experiment records, not independent expert or human gold.
Provider responses and qualified structural checks do not establish source truth
or deployment readiness. Research-methodology guidance informs the evaluation
design; its generic credibility scores are not runtime truth labels.

## Open questions

Which selector mechanism has a justified, falsifiable usefulness hypothesis?
Which exact paired task-completion criteria and sample size should be frozen?
Can a new naturally large, untouched corpus support both ranking and task-use
confirmation? Model checkpoint identity and source availability remain limits.
