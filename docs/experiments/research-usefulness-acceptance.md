# Research usefulness: prospective acceptance direction

Decision date: 2026-10-05. The maintainer selected research usefulness over
preserving incumbent URL identities. This document changes future evaluation
direction, not completed results. It registers no candidate, corpus, acquisition
or provider invocation.

## What changes

For future registered studies, replacing useful incumbent sources is allowed.
The count of displaced useful W0 top-ten IDs remains a descriptive diagnostic,
but the former at-most-one identity cap is no longer an adoption veto. Instead,
the selected evidence and resulting answer must preserve critical task coverage,
material contradictions and qualifications, and demonstrate research usefulness.
A different URL is not itself lost evidence; an apparently equivalent source is
not accepted without source-bound evidence of what it supports.

EXP-039 through EXP-076 retain their original protocols, analyses, failures and
rejections. EXP-076 also failed the unchanged +.05 mean-quality requirement.
It cannot be regraded, rerun or taken directly to confirmation under this decision.

## Requirements that remain

The separately approved [bounded release decision](research-usefulness-release-decision.md)
prospectively requires both-reference mean ranking gain >=.02 with positive
confidence lower bounds and explicit paired task-completion gains.
Per-case noninferiority, useful-source counts, declared facet retention, repeated
and rotated stability, exact navigation targets, acquisition completeness,
complete eligible membership, resource/latency limits and atomic native-order
fallback remain. A future study must freeze its exact numerical contract before
measurement. This direction authorizes no post-outcome threshold fitting.

Final confirmation still requires new tasks and new pools, independent blind
references frozen before selector outputs, naturally large pools, no omitted
cases or rescue acquisition, and preserved uncertainty. Brave remains 8/10 used;
this decision spends none. Ranking improvement alone is insufficient. Shared
SlopSearX behavior, X context propagation, cache/snapshot identity, compatibility,
security, integration, review and implementation PRs remain required.

## Evidence and task-use protections to freeze before the next study

1. Define each task's observable target, critical facets, required checks and
   legitimate abstention boundaries before acquisition. Distinguish discovery
   of a promising source from visible substantive evidence and task completion.
2. Capture bounded public source content once under a fixed policy; preserve
   failed/blocked/absent sources. Both arms use the same content/availability
   snapshot, consumer projection, opening budget, context and answerer contract.
3. Bind claims, qualifications and contradictions to immutable source IDs,
   content hashes and offsets. Validate references deterministically. Exact
   attribution proves identity, not entailment or correctness.
4. Two independent blind assessors establish evidence support and uncertainty
   before candidate outputs. They must distinguish opposing answers from
   differences of target, date, version or assumptions; copied publications
   are not independent corroboration. Do not manufacture consensus gold.
5. Assess selected-context coverage separately from answer use. Preserve
   evidence available but unused, evidence not delivered to an arm, and evidence
   unavailable in the acquired pool as separate failure categories.
6. Assess anonymized answers with the same critical-facet/target rules, require
   traceable application of evidence, and retain material contradictions and
   limits. Unsupported material claims, omitted critical qualifications or
   unearned completion claims cannot receive completion credit.
7. Freeze paired task-completion improvement and non-regression requirements
   for each independent assessor, with explicit treatment of disagreements,
   abstentions and unavailable evidence. Numerical thresholds, sample count
   and uncertainty rules must be prospectively registered before any new run;
   this policy document does not invent them after seeing outputs.

## Next executable stage

Develop and review a materially justified selector/measurement hypothesis under
this direction, then register its complete contract. Existing saved-result
diagnoses may motivate a hypothesis but cannot qualify it. No extra fusion
weight sweep, incumbent anchor or silent replay is authorized by this decision.
Only after development qualification may the frozen candidate enter untouched
confirmation, followed by full product implementation and verified PR delivery.
