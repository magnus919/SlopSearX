# W03 caller-directed adaptive research planning

The native planning service implements decomposition, identifier-preserving terminology variants, evidence-linked followup previews, all four deterministic default strategies, deadline parsing, and durable caller-directed research metadata. It reuses the accepted `SearchService`, one shared policy, tenant-scoped snapshots, authoritative `ResearchStore::claim_prepared` mutations, and `ResearchRunner::execute_claim`. It adds no provider-backed planner, autonomous sufficiency decision, or stopping policy.

`ssx-infra::research_planning::AdaptiveResearch` owns pure previews and admission. `ssx-mcp::research_planning::PlanningRegistry` composes the accepted search surface with all nine exact source descriptors and real authenticated HTTP, legacy SSE, and native stdio transport bindings. `planning-qualification` is an isolated fixture executable, never a production startup default. W09 still owns final production composition. The advertised `slopsearx_start_research.source` field explicitly rejects artifact composition before writes/dispatch until W08 projections and W09 integration exist; it is never silently ignored. This packet therefore does **not** claim complete nine-callable or production catalog parity.

## Authority and durable history

Caller-supplied plans always require the relevant specialist intent grant. Source-generated default templates preserve the source's internal `requires_intent_grant=false` distinction; callers cannot supply that field to bypass admission. Research, sensitive-engine, current engine eligibility, tenant, policy epoch, and captured-evidence rules still use the same current shared policy at preview, admission, recovery, and dispatch. The metadata-only research grant check does not authorize an unrelated unscoped engine list.

New followups validate their terminal parent attempt and admitted result IDs, reread current tenant-scoped snapshot expiry and provenance, and revalidate opaque authority inside the freshly loaded fenced callback before appending queries or spending execution budgets. Duplicate tagged query/scope pairs, resolved followups, stale/forged/cross-tenant references, malformed metadata, and changed progress or authority fail before new dispatch. Accepted continuation replay retains the existing history and charges, while rechecking the captured intent. Caller completion updates metadata without rewriting prior attempts/snapshots and preserves budget-exhaustion reasons. Native cancellation and recovery follow accepted W01/W02 joined-work and conservative-charge semantics.
